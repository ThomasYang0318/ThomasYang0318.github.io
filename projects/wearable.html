Smart Wearable Resistance Training System

IMUOptical SensingCNNFlutterLuckfox Pico Zero

A wearable system designed to analyze repetition count, movement quality, and fatigue-related physiological changes during resistance training, then present real-time feedback and post-training summaries through a mobile application.

Overview

The project combines motion sensing and optical sensing in a wrist-worn system. IMU data is used for exercise recognition, repetition segmentation, and movement-phase analysis, while optical measurements provide signals for fatigue estimation and physiological-change analysis. The application then presents live training feedback and a training summary.

Watch demo ↗ Presentation ↗

System architecture

The sensing pipeline connects the optical front end and IMU to a Luckfox Pico Zero embedded platform. Processed information is transmitted to a smartphone for training feedback.

ArchitectureOptical sensor + IMU → Luckfox Pico Zero → Smartphone

Hardware

The prototype includes the wearable enclosure, sensor board, optical sensing circuitry, IMU, and embedded computer. I also participated in sensor-module soldering and hardware testing.

HardwareSensor board · Optical front end · IMU · Embedded computer

AI & application

The motion-analysis workflow uses six-axis IMU signals and a CNN-based model to support phase-level movement analysis. The mobile interface is designed to make current training status and session summaries understandable at a glance.

AI pipelineSix-axis IMU → Segmentation → CNN classification → Movement analysis
ApplicationLive training status · Movement feedback · Session summary
